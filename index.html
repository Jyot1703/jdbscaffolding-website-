What we do

Scaffold for every stage of the build

From a single-storey renovation to a multi-level commercial site, JDB Group supplies and manages the access and edge protection your crew works off.

SERVICE / 01

Residential Scaffolding

SERVICE / 02

SERVICE / 03

Edge Protection & Void Barriers

Safety & compliance

Every lift, tagged

Every scaffold JDB Group erects carries a tag at the access point, so anyone on site can see its status at a glance — no guesswork before a crew climbs.

Green tag — in serviceInspected and safe to use as erected.
Red tag — out of serviceDo not use until re-inspected and cleared.

Compliance

01

Erected and maintained in line with AS/NZS 1576 scaffolding standards.

02

Work carried out to WorkSafe Victoria requirements for scaffolding and height safety.

03

Regular inspection intervals agreed with the builder for the duration of the job.

04

Documentation and tag records available on request for site audits.
